## Species Categories

**Default Species:** Found regularly throughout Selardor, with many coming to Merzileta from the East. These ancestries are usable by all players in the Frontier.

**Common Species:** Found only on or near the western continent of Okeron. These ancestries have been unlocked through in-game negotiations and diplomacy. These ancestries are usable by all players in the Frontier.

**Uncommon Species:** Newly discovered species found only in Okeron or on other Planes. These ancestries can only be used after a player has completed a specific mission to aid their people.

## Regarding Custom Lineage and Human Variant

!!! note "Human Variant"
    Due to 5.5e Human being the new ruleset's equivalent, and 5e feats are not allowed with 5.5e characters, **Human Variant is solely allowed for 5e Only player characters** [see Character Creation Rules for 5e Only PCs].

!!! note "Custom Lineage"
    While similar to Human Variant, **Custom Lineage** (found in *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*, Chapter 1) is available for 5.5e or Mixed PCs with the following changes:

    - Remove the Ability Score Increase.
    - Only 5.5e Origin Feats can be chosen for the species' given Feat.

For any player choosing this species, the image/description of the character's species must align with one of the Frontier Legal Species.
