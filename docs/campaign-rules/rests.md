# Long and Short Rests

The Hungry Frontier will use a **west march method** of Resting, this means that Long Rests may only be taken in the safety of town.

Rules for both Long Rests and Short Rests can be found in the PHB24 Rules Glossary, with the following changes:

- **Short Rests** can be taken any place of relative safety, and last anywhere from **1 hour** (within Dungeons) to **8 hours** (Day to Day).
- **Long Rests** can only be taken in the safety of town, and last anywhere from **8 hours** to **multiple days**.

After a session it is assumed your character immediately takes a long rest upon reaching town, the only exception is for player characters playing back to back sessions (same day and/or consecutive days).
