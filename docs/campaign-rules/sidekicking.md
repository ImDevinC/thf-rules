# Sidekicking

A Player Character may temporarily play at a higher level in a session by **sidekicking**. In order to sidekick, a side kicking player character must have a **Mentor**, another player character at least 3 levels higher.

- While mentored the side kick uses the **proficiency bonus of the Mentor**. This will generally affect most d20 Tests.
- The side kick also gains a number of hit dice, of their own class, up to their **Mentor's level minus 1**. They may take the Fixed value (shown in PHB24 → Level Advancement) or roll these Hit Dice and add their Constitution modifier a number of times equal to the number of Hit Dice rolled.

For the side kick to keep these bonuses, the Side Kick and their Mentor must stay within **180 ft** of each other.

!!! example "Worked Example"
    **Mentor** — Wizard Lvl 9, proficiency bonus: `+4`
    **Side Kick** — Fighter Lvl 4, hit dice: `d10`, `+2` CON modifier

    While within 180 ft of the wizard, the fighter's proficiency bonus becomes `+4` instead of their normal `+2`.

    The fighter also gains 4 more hit dice and 4× their CON modifier. In this case it would be `4d10 + 8`. Using the fixed value for fighters, the Side Kick gains `(6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 8) = 32 HP` for that session.

## Class Hit Dice

| Class                                          | Hit Dice | Average |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------- |
| Barbarian                                      | d12      | 7       |
| Fighter, Paladin, Ranger                       | d10      | 6       |
| Bard, Cleric, Druid, Monk, Rogue, Warlock      | d8       | 5       |
| Sorcerer, Wizard                               | d6       | 4       |

## Companions, Summoned Creatures, and Similar — Update 02.07.2026

!!! info "Campaign Rule Addition"
    Regarding Animal Companions, Summoned Creatures and similar Companions that rely on a Player Character's level and/or proficiency bonus.

When a Player Character is side kicked up by a mentor (see Side kicking Rules above) and that PC has a companion that uses the PC's proficiency bonus (PB) or level for its stats (e.g., Artificer-Steel Defender, Ranger-Animal Companion, etc.), the side kicking PC uses their **side-kicked PB or Level** (mentor's minus 1) to determine that companion's stats.
