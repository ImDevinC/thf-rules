# Waiver of Session XP

Want to keep your character within a certain level range? Are all your friends just now joining the Frontier? Do you just really like how a peculiar Guide runs Intro sessions? Or do the deadly challenges that face a lower level character call like a siren to you?

For whatever reason, if you do not want to be rewarded XP after a session, you must let that Session's Guide know **before the end of the session** and you part ways. In doing this you are waiving the XP reward for that session:

- It does **not** get stored, tracked or banked for later use.
- It cannot be applied to another player character.
- It is lost to the Wild Frontier, gone for good.

In this way you can stay at whatever level you wish, for however long you wish.
