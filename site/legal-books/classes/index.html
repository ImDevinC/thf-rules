    Classes & Subclasses of the Frontier¶

    Formatting Conventions

    Bold subclass names are 5.5e subclasses, including 5.5e subclasses that share a name with a 5e subclass.

    If making a 5.5e player character, subclasses from Frontier Legal 5e sourcebooks are available to use unless a 5.5e equivalent is available.
